Browser sharing is available when the build is configured with a tunnel endpoint. After you confirm **Remote control** in the extension, a secret link gives the recipient access to every enabled tab and lets them open new tabs. That can include screenshots, page content, URLs, network data, cookies, and browser storage. Only share with someone you trust.

In the toolbar, open **Remote ON** and choose **Stop sharing** to revoke the link. Closing or detaching the tab where sharing started, or closing Chrome, also ends access. The recipient's new tabs detach and stay open.

Keep the link out of logs and messages to anyone outside the intended recipients. The service operator can handle browser data and connection metadata. Check the service's privacy policy before sharing.

For an authenticated connection to a relay you run yourself, see [Remote access](./remote-access.mdx).
